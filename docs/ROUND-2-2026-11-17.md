# Round 2: hero, three-step form, shorter pages, plainer copy (2026-11-17)

Feedback from Andy (reviewed on a phone) and Tony: the site felt too vertical, the hero image took up too much of the screen, the before/after should be its own proof section, some copy felt tacky ("No job too small", the East Chicago "plan to built" story), and the projects and services sections repeated the same photos. Pages and services stay as they are for now.

## Changes

- **Home opening:** the East Chicago photo now sits behind the headline with a dark overlay. Desktop shows the headline, a call/text button and the three-step request form side by side in the first screen. Phones show the photo with the headline and two buttons (Free consultation, Call), then the form directly below. No video, to protect load speed.
- **Three-step form** (home and `/contact`): 1) project type as tap tiles, 2) city or ZIP plus optional timing and property type, 3) name, phone or email, optional note. Without JavaScript all three steps show as one form. The note is now optional; a new optional `timing` field is validated on the server and included in the office email. "Best way to reach you" was removed from the form (the server still accepts it).
- **Home page:** 10 sections reduced to 6 (opening, recent projects, services, proof, company and process, contact). About 40% shorter on desktop and phones. Service areas remain in the menu and footer.
- **Recent projects:** photos only, no captions. A swipeable row on phones, a grid on desktop.
- **Services:** cards keep their descriptions. On phones they are compact rows (photo left, text right). "Other services" shows the first eight trades on phones plus "All services".
- **Proof section:** before/after slider beside the Google rating, with three reviews below (a swipeable row on phones). Doug's overlay card is removed from the home page.
- **Process:** three plain steps beside the family section. The plan-to-built drawing and its component are removed.
- **Copy:** "No job too small", "no minimum project size", "see for yourself", "starts on paper" and similar lines replaced with plain wording. Page-hero labels that repeated the breadcrumb were removed.
- **Spacing:** section padding reduced site-wide.
- **Contact page on phones:** the form comes first.
