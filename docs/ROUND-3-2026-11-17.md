# Round 3: calmer opening, one set of contact buttons, trust first (2026-11-17)

Feedback from Tony after reviewing round 2 on a phone: the mobile opening repeated call and consultation buttons three times, followed straight away by the form, which felt like too much. The desktop photo was too zoomed in. "Other services" looked clunky, and the contact page form sat too low.

## Changes

- **Opening (all sizes):** shorter headline, "Family-owned construction and remodeling since 1990". The location and "Licensed and insured" are in the line above it, and the paragraph is one sentence. The stars and the "licensed and insured" row are removed from the opening.
- **Opening on desktop:** the photo is a framed panel close to its own 4:3 shape, beside the form, so it is no longer over-cropped or stretched. The photo is served at its real size (up to 1024 px). One "Call or text" button remains.
- **Opening on phones:** photo and headline only, with no buttons and no form. The bottom bar (Call, Text, Free consultation) is the single set of actions. The red call icon in the header is hidden on phones because the bar already has Call. It still shows on tablets, which have no bar.
- **Home order:** opening, proof (before/after, Google rating, licensed / family-owned / free consultation, reviews), recent projects, services, family and process, contact.
- **Form on phones:** the home page form is in the contact section at the bottom, after the proof. On desktop it stays in the opening, and the contact section links up to it. Both use the same three-step component.
- **Other services:** the 25 chips are replaced with six plain rows: Interiors, Exteriors, Structure & site, HVAC/plumbing/electrical, Repairs & restoration, and Commercial & multifamily. Each row has a one-line description and links to its page. They show as 3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablets and 1 on phones.
- **Contact page:** the form now starts in the first screen, with the heading on the left and the form on the right on desktop. On phones the order is heading, form, then call/text/email, office and financing.
