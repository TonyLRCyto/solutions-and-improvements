# Service-area launch plan

Reviewed October 28, 2026. The owner wants more Illinois communities and priority for places with stronger household finances within the confirmed service footprint.

## Recommended scope

Plan for **12 useful community pages: six Indiana and six Illinois**, plus the two existing regional pages and the service-area directory. This is an editorial starting point, not an SEO-required page count or a ranking promise. The broader footprint stays visible: Lake and Porter counties through La Porte and South Bend, and the southern Chicago suburbs from Lansing to Mokena.

The preview now includes all 12 communities. Existing city URLs are preserved. Added Indiana pages: Schererville and Winfield. Added Illinois pages: Orland Park, Tinley Park, Homewood and Flossmoor.

## Content added to the preview

Each city page now has a short planning section with its own municipality resource and a practical point to discuss at the consultation. Public text summarizes planning considerations; it does not reproduce fee tables, promise approval, guarantee permit schedules or claim completed local projects. Links lead to official municipal guidance:

- [Munster remodel and alteration application](https://www.munster.org/egov/apps/document/center.egov?id=3822&view=item): relevant application scope and existing plans.
- [St. John Building and Planning](https://stjohnin.gov/building-and-planning/): project categories and subdivision/HOA considerations.
- [Dyer building and zoning application](https://www.townofdyer.com/Portals/0/Content/Forms/Building_Permit_Application.pdf): property, subdivision and scope details.
- [Crown Point inspection guidance](https://www.crownpoint.in.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2000/Crown-Point-Inspections_Updated-2024): coordination of building and trade inspections.
- [Schererville inspection checklist](https://www.schererville.org/egov/apps/document/center.egov?id=123&view=item): inspection stages, plans and site access.
- [Winfield concrete application](https://www.winfield.in.gov/media/2956): survey, work location, setbacks and drainage/site information.
- [Mokena construction information](https://www.mokena.gov/pview.aspx?catid=0&id=20806): permit types and scope planning.
- [Frankfort residential construction guide](https://www.frankfortil.org/Residential%20construction%20guidelines%20updated%203.03.2022.pdf): new-build documentation and applicable subdivision approval.
- [Orland Park permit information](https://www.orlandpark.org/departments/development-services/building-permit-information): commercial exterior review alongside interior scope.
- [Tinley Park permit and remodel guides](https://www.tinleypark.org/government/departments/community_development/building/building_permits.php): kitchen/bath guides and trade changes.
- [Homewood building information](https://www.village.homewood.il.us/how-do-i/apply-for/building-permits?navid=157): temporary dumpsters, trees and site logistics.
- [Flossmoor project fact sheets](https://www.flossmoor.org/156/Permit-Fact-Sheets-Applications): project-specific documents and applicable HOA approval.

Several municipalities limit automated fetches or redirect PDF links. Their current indexed official guidance supplied the facts above; recheck the live resource and applicable process while planning the actual job. Do not promote these summaries into definitive permitting advice.

## Launch readiness

**All 12 city pages remain drafts with `indexReady: false`.** They are visible on the noindex preview but remain excluded from a production build and production sitemap until reviewed. The two regional pages remain the broad geographic landing pages for launch. There is no automatic decision to launch all 12 city pages merely because the preview contains them.

For each city, confirm the actual coverage and current contractor registration wording with Andy, review the local planning summary, then add useful first-hand detail: a confirmed local project, an approved project example with an accurate location/scope, a local customer question with a substantive answer, or another owner-verified local planning insight. Photos can be reused from real projects with accurate captions; never relabel a project as being in a city without confirmation. A local project photograph is useful evidence, not an absolute technical prerequisite.

Release city pages individually when they provide value beyond a city-name substitution. Set `draft: false` and `indexReady: true` together after review, then check internal links, titles, canonicals and sitemap membership in the production build. Keep broad coverage on regional pages even where a dedicated city page is deferred. Do not add a service-by-city grid of near-duplicate pages.

This follows Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [doorway-abuse policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#doorway-abuse). No official minimum page count or word count underlies the recommendation.
